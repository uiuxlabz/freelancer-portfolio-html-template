My Story

8+ Years of Crafting Digital Experiences

I'm a multidisciplinary freelancer who thrives at the intersection of design and code. What started as a curiosity about how websites are built turned into a deep passion for creating digital products that people genuinely enjoy using.

From early-stage startups to established brands, I've helped teams ship products that are fast, accessible, and beautiful. Every project is an opportunity to solve a real problem with thoughtful design and clean engineering.

When I'm not at the keyboard, you'll find me hiking trails, brewing pour-over coffee, or sketching UI concepts in my notebook. I believe the best work comes from a curious, well-rounded mind.
